# Changelog

## 1.0

- Log blood sugar, food, and drinks from Today. Tap the displayed date or time to change it.
- Receive an Android reminder to log blood sugar 30 minutes after recording food or drink with the current time.
- Add daily notes, browse diary history, and view blood sugar charts.
- Edit personal details and manage weight measurements from Profile.
- Switch between English and Persian in Settings.
- Back up the diary on the device and import or export diary data.
